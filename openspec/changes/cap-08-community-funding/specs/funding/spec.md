## Purpose

How Nah? is paid for without selling anything about the people in it, and what supporting it does and does not change.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Free to use, in full

Everyone in Nah? SHALL have every feature without paying, and nothing SHALL be locked behind a payment, a subscription or a supporter status (ruled 2026-09-15).

#### Scenario: A new person

- **WHEN** a person joins Nah?
- **THEN** every feature is available to them, and nothing asks them to pay

#### Scenario: Someone who never donates

- **WHEN** a person never supports Nah?
- **THEN** nothing in the app is missing, degraded, or asks them to reconsider

### Requirement: Paid for by donations, outside the app

Nah? SHALL be paid for by voluntary donations, made on its site through a donation platform. The app SHALL NOT take payments of any kind (ruled 2026-09-15).

#### Scenario: Wanting to support Nah?

- **WHEN** someone wants to support Nah?
- **THEN** the site tells them how, and the donation is made on a donation platform

#### Scenario: Inside the app

- **WHEN** the app is inspected
- **THEN** it contains no payment, in-app purchase or subscription

### Requirement: Supporting changes nothing anyone can see

Supporting Nah? SHALL change nothing that any other person can see in Nah?: no badge, no mark beside a name, no theme others can see, and no list or ranking of supporters. Nah? SHALL NOT link a donation to a person in Nah? (ruled 2026-09-15).

#### Scenario: A supporter in a circle

- **WHEN** Maya donates to Nah?
- **THEN** nobody in her circle can tell from anything in Nah?

#### Scenario: A donation arrives

- **WHEN** Nah? receives a donation
- **THEN** nothing connects it to any person's place in Nah?

### Requirement: Public money, a public roadmap, and nothing for sale

Nah? SHALL publish what it receives and what it spends it on, and SHALL keep a public roadmap to which anyone can suggest something. No feature SHALL be voted on, ranked by a count, or funded by pledges (ADR-0004, ruled 2026-09-15).

#### Scenario: Looking at the money

- **WHEN** anyone looks at Nah?'s finances
- **THEN** they find what came in and what it paid for

#### Scenario: Suggesting something

- **WHEN** someone suggests a feature
- **THEN** it can be read and answered, with no vote count or pledge attached to it

### Requirement: No money that needs growth

Nah? SHALL NOT take investment that requires it to grow, to be sold, or to earn a return from the people in it.

#### Scenario: An offer of investment

- **WHEN** investment is offered on terms that need Nah? to grow or be sold
- **THEN** it is refused
