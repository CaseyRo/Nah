# Funding

## Why

Nah? has to pay for its servers without selling anything about the people in it (`product-principles`). The January version mixed donations with supporter badges, exclusive themes, digital goods, a voting roadmap and crowdfunded features. Inside a circle, a badge is status; a vote is a count; and a pledge turns the roadmap into a market. On 2026-09-15 the ruling was donations only, with nothing a supporter gets that anyone else can see.

## What Changes

- **funding**:
  - Nah? is free to use in full, with nothing behind a payment
  - it is paid for by voluntary donations, made outside the app
  - supporting Nah? changes nothing anyone can see, and no donation is linked to a person in Nah?
  - what Nah? receives and spends, and its roadmap, are public; anyone can suggest something, and nothing is voted on or bought
  - no investment that needs growth or an exit
- **Removed from the January version**: supporter badges and exclusive themes, sticker packs and other digital goods, feature voting, crowdfunded features, and donations inside the app.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `funding`: how Nah? is paid for, and what supporting it does and does not change.

### Modified Capabilities

None: `openspec/specs/` holds no specs yet.

## Impact

- The site: a page for supporting Nah?, the public finances and the roadmap.
- `apps/mobile`: no payment code of any kind.
- `apps/server`: nothing.
- Earlier direction: the paid hosted service recommended in the September audit is no longer the plan. Hosting for other people (M7, CDI-1873) is paid for by donations too.
- Linear: no issue covers funding yet.
